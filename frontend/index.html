Tomato rasam

Serves 4 · 25 minutes

Made with Spice Mama Rasam Powder

You need

  • A lime-sized ball of tamarind, soaked in 2 cups warm water
  • 2 ripe tomatoes, crushed by hand
  • 1½ tsp rasam powder, ¼ tsp turmeric powder, salt
  • ½ cup thin cooked toor dal water
  • Ghee, mustard seeds, 2 dried red chillies, curry leaves, a pinch of hing, coriander leaves

Method

  1. Simmer the tamarind water with tomatoes, turmeric and salt for 8–10 minutes.
  2. Add the dal water and rasam powder. Heat until it froths at the edges; don’t let it boil hard.
  3. Fry mustard seeds, chillies, curry leaves and hing in ghee, pour over, and finish with coriander.

Mixed vegetable sambar

Serves 4 · 40 minutes

Made with Spice Mama Sambar Powder

You need

  • ½ cup toor dal, cooked soft with turmeric
  • 2 cups vegetables: drumstick, potato, shallots
  • A small ball of tamarind, soaked and squeezed
  • 2 tbsp sambar powder, salt
  • Oil, mustard seeds, a few fenugreek seeds, dried red chilli, curry leaves, hing

Method

  1. Cook the vegetables in the tamarind water with salt until just tender.
  2. Stir in the sambar powder, then the mashed dal. Simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Pour over a tempering of mustard, fenugreek, chilli, curry leaves and hing.

Aloo methi fry

Serves 3 · 25 minutes

Made with Spice Mama Kasuri Methi and Guntur Gold

You need

  • 3 potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tbsp kasuri methi, crushed between your palms
  • 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp turmeric, salt
  • 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp cumin seeds

Method

  1. Crackle cumin in hot oil, add potatoes and turmeric, cover and cook on low until tender.
  2. Add chilli powder, coriander powder and salt; fry until the edges crisp.
  3. Scatter the kasuri methi in the last minute and toss.
